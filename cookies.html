Cookie Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, how AutoGlob uses them, and the choices you have.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the site function properly, remember your preferences, and provide information to the site owner about how the site is used.

Storage used by the current site

AutoGlob stores your cookie preference in your browser's local storage under the key autoglob_cookie_consent. The current site does not load Google Analytics or AdSense.

External services

The site loads some images from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. The VIN decoder connects to NHTSA when you request a vehicle lookup. Those providers receive the connection information needed to deliver their services.

Managing your preference

You can clear AutoGlob's site data in your browser settings to remove the stored preference and display the choice again.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. Any changes will be posted here with a revised date.

Questions?

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please use the AutoGlob GitHub issue tracker.
