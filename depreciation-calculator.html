Real depreciation varies enormously by brand, model, mileage, condition and market demand. Luxury and EV models often depreciate faster; some limited-production cars appreciate. Treat this as a rough guideline only.

Typical depreciation curve

Most new cars lose roughly 20-25% of their value in the first year, then around 10-15% per year after that as the rate of decline slows. This calculator applies a simplified version of that curve: a steeper drop in years one through three, tapering to a gentler decline afterward.
