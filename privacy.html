Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AutoGlob ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit AutoGlob or use any related services.

Information and site services

AutoGlob does not currently offer email subscriptions or run Google Analytics or AdSense. We store your theme and cookie-banner choices locally in your browser. Hosting and external services may process connection information when your browser requests their resources.

How the current site works

Local storage remembers your light/dark theme and cookie-banner choice. Those choices do not activate analytics or advertising. The VIN decoder sends the VIN you submit to NHTSA's public vPIC API; AutoGlob does not save it. Google Fonts requests connect your browser to Google's font services. Site article images are hosted with AutoGlob.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited data with service providers who help us operate the site — such as hosting and external resource providers — solely for the purpose of providing those services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, and to opt out of certain data processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

AutoGlob is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the AutoGlob GitHub issue tracker. Do not include private information in a public issue.
