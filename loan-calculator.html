How car loan payments are calculated

Monthly payment = P × [r(1+r)^n] / [(1+r)^n − 1], where P is the loan principal (price minus down payment), r is the monthly interest rate (APR ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments. This standard amortization formula is what most lenders use as their baseline.
