## Rights decisions

Authorize an original image only with asset-specific evidence covering AutoGlob's intended publication:

- An applicable explicit editorial-use permission from the creator/rightsholder or brand pressroom, including monetized editorial use where relevant.
- A commercial-compatible open license applying to this image, with all attribution/share-alike/no-derivatives conditions satisfied. NC restrictions do not authorize this commercial project. Do not assume a site-wide license covers third-party photographs.
- A verified public-domain basis for this specific image and intended jurisdiction/use.
- Written or purchased permission with an applicable scope; do not send requests or pay without the user's authorization.

Editorial-only permissions may allow editorial coverage on a monetized site while prohibiting advertising/endorsement. Read the actual terms rather than treating all monetized editorial use as advertising or all pressroom media as unrestricted.

If rights are unknown, record that status and find another authorized original or omit the image. Do not hotlink, crop out watermarks, regenerate the photograph or relabel it as a substitute for permission. Retain original frames and markings. Technical compression/resizing/conversion must fit the permission; do not change location, color, components or scene.
