Analyze each article and choose its useful, supported images. There is no fixed image count or mandatory front/interior/rear/console sequence. Never invent a concept interior or unseen component to fill a template. Keep each depicted vehicle's identity and color consistent with its actual references.

Save lasting corrections in the skill and project rules. Do not turn a one-article example into a universal layout. Keep Tools calculator-only, preserve both themes, and never add TTN Finance analytics to AutoGlob.


AutoGlob is a commercial publication. Use experienced automotive journalistic judgment, original analysis and verified facts; no invented drives, quotes or people. Preserve good existing imagery during text/SEO fixes. Use Car and Driver selectively for inspiration, not as a catalog of sections to copy. Present necessary sources compactly without numbered prose markers.

Required reporting sources are https://www.caranddriver.com/ , https://www.motortrend.com/ and https://www.topgear.com/ . Use these publications for news discovery; source technical and performance figures only from official brands. Publish licensed original photos without semantic alterations; if official-photo publication permission is unavailable, reference-based generation is an authorized fallback. Do not add a public AI label; retain provenance internally and never call generated work an official photo. Verify rights per image for monetized editorial use and preserve required credits. Exclude media test measurements and verdicts. Write fresh fact-based original analysis with required news/image attribution, not close paraphrases of complete articles. Read `.codex/skills/autoglob-editorial/references/sources-and-image-rights.md` for collection/rights decisions. This clarification supersedes older generation-first rules.

Use the approved News / Analysis / Guides / Stories / Tools architecture. Keep all-articles.html as the complete archive. Preserve old article URLs and approved imagery. Read the latest architecture rules in the skill reference.
