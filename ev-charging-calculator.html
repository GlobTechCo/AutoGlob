Why charging costs more than the sticker kWh number

Charging an EV isn't 100% efficient — some energy is lost as heat during AC-to-DC conversion at the charger and battery. Home Level 2 charging typically runs at roughly 85-90% efficiency, meaning you pay for more kWh from the wall than actually end up stored in the battery.
