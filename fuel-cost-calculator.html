Actual consumption varies with driving style, terrain, load, and weather. Use manufacturer fuel economy figures as a starting estimate, not a guarantee.

Why your real mileage varies

Official fuel economy ratings are measured under standardized lab conditions. Real-world consumption is typically 10-20% higher in city driving, cold weather, or with a heavy right foot, and can be lower on steady highway cruising with good aerodynamics.
