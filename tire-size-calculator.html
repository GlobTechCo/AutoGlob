Diameter is calculated from standard tire geometry (width × aspect ratio × 2 + rim diameter). Always confirm fitment with your vehicle's manual or a tire shop before buying.

How tire size math works

A tire size like 225/45R17 breaks down as: 225mm section width, 45% aspect ratio (sidewall height as a percentage of width), mounted on a 17-inch rim. Overall diameter = rim diameter + (2 × sidewall height), where sidewall height = width × (aspect ratio / 100).

Changing tire diameter affects your speedometer reading and odometer accuracy, since both are calibrated for the original tire's revolutions per mile. A diameter increase makes your speedometer under-read your real speed; a decrease makes it over-read.
