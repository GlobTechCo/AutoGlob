About AutoGlob

Time & Road — Stories Behind The Machines

AutoGlob is a commercial automotive publication for readers who want to understand cars, ownership costs and the history behind the machines. We cover new-model news, analysis of manufacturer specifications, ownership guides and historical features. Our seven driver tools help turn practical questions into estimates you can inspect.

How we research and write

We use Car and Driver, MotorTrend and Top Gear to discover current news topics, then verify vehicle technical and performance figures in official manufacturer sources. We preserve model-year, market, configuration and estimate or preliminary qualifications. Calculators use separately explained methods and hypothetical inputs.

AutoGlob Staff is the editorial byline for this work. We do not conduct road tests or publish measurements from media road tests. Our Analysis section explains officially published brand specifications. Sources appear in a compact expandable block at the end of each article.

Images and corrections

We source original photographs from official brand pressrooms and check publication permissions. When suitable publication rights are unavailable, we may create reference-based illustrations. Image choice follows each story, and vehicle specifications always come from official manufacturer sources.

Publication dates show the original release; modification dates identify updates. We correct factual errors and retain research and image-reference records in the project documentation.

Commercial transparency

AutoGlob is being developed for monetization. The current site does not run Google Analytics or AdSense. Any future sponsorship, advertising or affiliate content must be clearly identified, and the policies must describe the services actually in use.

Contact and corrections

Send public corrections and suggestions through the AutoGlob issue tracker. Do not post personal or private information in a public issue.
