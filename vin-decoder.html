How VIN decoding works

Every vehicle sold since 1981 has a unique 17-character VIN, usually found on the dashboard (visible through the windshield), the driver's side door jamb, or the vehicle's registration and insurance documents. The first character indicates the country of manufacture, characters 2-3 the manufacturer, and characters 4-8 encode the vehicle's brand, engine and body type. The 10th character encodes the model year.

This tool queries the NHTSA's public vPIC (Vehicle Product Information Catalog) API directly from your browser — no data is sent to or stored on AutoGlob's servers.
